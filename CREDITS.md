# Sources and acknowledgements

The per-location records in `data/descriptions/` (repository) or `sources/` (built mod) identify consulted Wikipedia pages, pinned revisions, source languages and access times. Wikipedia contributors are credited through those page links and their contribution histories. These records are research provenance, not an assertion that Wikipedia endorses the mod or that each statement has been independently verified.

`revision_url` points to the consulted revision and `history_url` to its contributors. Credit the Wikipedia contributors identified by those histories and the **EU5 Location Descriptions contributors** for the project's editorial work. The descriptions condense, reorganize and translate consulted material with AI assistance; changes have been made and the source pages are not reproduced unchanged. Those notices are not removed by this project license. Page-level exceptional notices and protected quotations have not received an exhaustive review. The existence of a citation is not proof that all rights questions or all facts have been independently checked.
