Location Descriptions for Every Tile — 1.0 released

Every location without vanilla flavour text now has a description: 28,254 towns, villages, mountains, lakes and sea zones, in English and Simplified Chinese in one mod. The 257 vanilla descriptions are left as they are.

I'm a geography enthusiast; the texts were drafted with AI assistance from published sources and corrected and checked by me as carefully as I could. With this many entries some mistakes will remain. If a place, date or translation looks wrong, a comment with the place name and a source is the most helpful. Either language is fine.
