[h1]A description for every tile on the map[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13578589985414708755/92635B0C7AA1ADBF48CF4D76AFF2CDF9C6F08F3D/[/img]

Vanilla EU5 describes only a few hundred locations.

This mod adds high-quality descriptions to all the remaining [b]28,316[/b] tiles: towns, countryside, mountains, lakes and seas. Impassable tiles are covered too!

The [b]257[/b] tiles that already have a vanilla description keep it word for word, followed by one or two new paragraphs on their history during the game's era.

[list]
[*]Supports English and Chinese
[*]Keeps EU5's flavour
[*]Keeps the vanilla descriptions and continues them
[*]Can be added to an existing save; see mod compatibility below
[*]Achievement compatible
[/list]

Every description is based on local-language sources, mainly Wikipedia, and went through several rounds of fact-checking. I used the best models available, went through a huge number of tokens and spent quite a bit of money. But for this mod, I think it was worth it :)

I'm a geography and history enthusiast, and I've corrected and checked these entries as carefully as I could. If you find a mistake, please leave a comment. I'll keep maintaining and updating this mod!

[h2]Mod compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3721193233][b]Oikoumene: Dynamic Place Names[/b][/url]: Both mods provide English descriptions for 578 of the same locations. When both are enabled, Oikoumene's descriptions will take precedence. No other known conflicts.
[/list]

Support the other official EU5 languages (French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Korean). I'll add them as soon as I can. If you'd like anything else, just leave a comment or contact me.
