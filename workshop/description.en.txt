[h1]A description for every place on the map[/h1]
EU5 ships with flavour text for only a few hundred locations. This mod fills in the rest: 28,254 short descriptions of the towns, countryside, mountains, lakes and sea zones you click on every day, written in the style of the vanilla texts and focused on 1337–1837.

[list]
[*]English and Simplified Chinese in one download.
[*]Major cities get two or three paragraphs of local history; small places and sea zones get a short note on their landscape, people and past.
[*]The 257 vanilla descriptions are left untouched.
[*]Text only: no gameplay, map or name changes.
[/list]

[h2]About these texts[/h2]
I'm a geography enthusiast. The descriptions were drafted with AI assistance from published sources, mostly Wikipedia in English and the local language, and I have corrected and checked them as carefully as I could, with city entries checked fact by fact. With more than 28,000 entries, mistakes will remain. If you spot one, please leave a comment with the place name and, if you can, a source. English or Chinese is fine.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Made for game version 1.3. Mods that rewrite the same location descriptions will conflict; load whichever you prefer last. Map overhauls that rename or add locations will leave those places without text.
