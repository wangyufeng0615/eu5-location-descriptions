[h1]A description for every tile on the map[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13578589985414708755/92635B0C7AA1ADBF48CF4D76AFF2CDF9C6F08F3D/[/img]

Vanilla EU5 describes only a few hundred locations. This mod adds English and Chinese descriptions to all the other [b]28,316[/b] tiles: towns, countryside, mountains, lakes and seas. The [b]257[/b] tiles that already have a vanilla description keep it word for word, followed by new paragraphs on their history during the game's era.

⭐ [b]Strongly recommended: subscribe to the other two EU5 mods as well.[/b] Together the three cover the map, its people and its countries.
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3833397845][b]Descriptions for Every Historical Character[/b][/url]: biographies for 7,789 historical characters, shown on hover
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3838927963][b]Descriptions Expanded: Countries, Cultures, Dynasties & More[/b][/url]: over 7,000 descriptions for countries, cultures, dynasties, religions and more
[/list]
Or subscribe to all three at once from [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3838947253][b]the collection[/b][/url].

[h2]Update history[/h2]
[list]
[*]1.2.3: Removed the English copies for other game languages added in 1.2.2; the download is back to its earlier size.
[*]1.2.2: Other game languages (French, German, Spanish and more) now show the English descriptions.
[*]1.2.1: Reworked 297 city and town descriptions that read like chronicles; rewrote Niani to separate legend from record; fixed a few errors such as the founding date of Antalya. Based on player feedback.
[*]1.2.0: The 257 vanilla descriptions are kept word for word and continued with the place's history during the game's era; four vanilla tiles now name their later city.
[*]1.1.3: Noted Astoria, corrected Salem, Wilmington and Dover, removed stray symbols from 18 southern African entries and translated English names left in about 200 Chinese entries. Based on player feedback.
[*]1.1.2: Packaged without the new data by mistake; its changes took effect in 1.1.3.
[*]1.1.1: Corrected the later-city lines for Seattle and Olympia; 31 tiles that are themselves cities now name their later city; rewrote Chiquimula.
[*]1.1.0: 788 tiles with tribal or indigenous names now name the well-known later city that lies there, such as Denver. Suggested by a player.
[*]1.0.9: Revised 50 high-profile tiles after a source check, rewriting Xiangyang, Agra, Malacca and three more; corrected about 40 other entries.
[*]1.0.8: Corrected ten descriptions about the wrong place or with unsupported local stories.
[*]1.0.7: Corrected 19 descriptions whose subject did not match the tile, including Bone in Sulawesi.
[*]1.0.6: Fixed ten tiles that described a different place with a similar name, such as Baofeng and Büren.
[*]1.0.5: Wuchang now describes the old county of modern Ezhou; reworked Jiangxia. Based on player feedback.
[*]1.0.4: Rewrote Wuxi: the tin-mine origin of its name is a later folk story. Based on player feedback.
[*]1.0.3: Corrected the name of the Wallerfanger blue pigment. Based on player feedback.
[*]1.0.2: Restored lost paragraph breaks and fixed spacing, wording and stray notes in several hundred entries.
[*]1.0.1: Added 62 missing tiles such as Porto Santo and part of the Ligurian Sea; new title and cover. Based on player feedback.
[*]1.0.0: First public release.
[/list]
Details for each version: [url=https://github.com/wangyufeng0615/eu5-location-descriptions/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md]full changelog[/url].
